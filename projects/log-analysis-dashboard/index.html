Log Analysis Dashboard_

Investigate synthetic authentication events using transparent detection rules. Review evidence, test false-positive scenarios, and export an incident report. All analysis runs in this browser tab; no network requests are made.

Learning lab only: alerts are investigation leads, not proof of compromise. Do not paste passwords, tokens, personal information, or logs you are not authorised to handle.

Detection rules

AUTH-01 · Repeated failures
3+ failed logins from one source within 5 minutes.
AUTH-02 · Multiple accounts
One source attempts 3+ distinct accounts within 10 minutes.
AUTH-03 · Multiple sources
One account receives failures from 3+ source IPs within 10 minutes.
AUTH-04 · Success after failures
A successful login follows 3+ failures for the same account and source within 15 minutes.
IAM-01 · Privilege change
A privilege-change event is flagged for review, not automatically treated as malicious.

5. Analyst notes & investigation conclusion

Document what the evidence supports, what remains unknown, and what should happen next. These fields are included in exported reports.

Keep notes factual. Do not claim a compromise is confirmed unless independent evidence supports that conclusion.

Analyst checklist

  • Confirm the time range and whether the source/account activity is expected.
  • Check surrounding events, identity context, MFA, and any approved testing or maintenance.
  • Record evidence and reasoning; note false-positive possibilities.
  • Follow your organisation's incident process before blocking accounts or addresses.
