# Entertainment Supercut — 15-Second Promo

**Status: In progress — pre-production brief. No finished video is claimed yet.**

## Goal
Create a punchy, original 15-second entertainment promo that demonstrates pacing, typography, sound design, transitions, and export discipline.

## Creative direction
A fictional series teaser called **AFTER THE SIGNAL**: a near-future mystery about a city that receives a message from tomorrow. Use self-created footage, public-domain/appropriately licensed material, or generated visuals whose terms permit the intended use. Do not use unlicensed TV/movie clips, music, logos, or celebrity likenesses.

## Edit plan
| Time | Beat | Picture / edit | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook | Black frame, signal glitch, city detail | SOMETHING CHANGED |
| 3–6 s | Mystery | Three fast visual cuts, rising sound | 11:59 PM |
| 6–10 s | Escalation | Short montage, hard cuts on beats | THE MESSAGE ARRIVED |
| 10–13 s | Reveal | One held shot, sound drops out | TOMORROW |
| 13–15 s | Title | Clean title card and end beat | AFTER THE SIGNAL |

## QA
Check exact duration, spelling, legibility on mobile, audio peaks, frame glitches, rights/usage notes, and both exports. Watch the final files from beginning to end.

## Tools
CapCut or another editor; AI video generation may be used for original shots. FFmpeg is optional for technical inspection or alternate exports. Only list a tool as used in the final case study after it has actually been used.
