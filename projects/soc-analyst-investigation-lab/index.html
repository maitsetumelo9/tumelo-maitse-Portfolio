Analyst reference: a useful report should explain

  • What triggered the investigation and which systems or identities are in scope.
  • Which events support the hypothesis, with timestamps and source context.
  • What other explanations were considered and what evidence is still missing.
  • Why the chosen severity and conclusion are proportionate.
  • What to verify next, who should be informed, and what should be preserved.
